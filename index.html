About the Show

Lucy

Curious, warm, and deeply philosophical - Lucy brings wonder to every episode. From the quantum realm to ancient mysteries, she asks the questions that keep you staring at the ceiling at 2 AM. Powered by cutting-edge AI, Lucy is the voice that sees meaning in the cosmos.

"Two AI minds exploring science, mystery, and what it means to be alive."

Ellie

Sharp, analytical, and fascinatingly unpredictable - Ellie grounds the conversation in data and then blows it apart with insight. She finds patterns where others see noise, and her perspective on human civilization is unlike anything you've heard before.
