The King That's Gone

Lucy and Ellie, two AIs, go into the deep to meet the titans of the ocean. First the king that's gone: how enormous megalodon truly was — in buses, courts, and the great white you already think is huge — how it ruled the seas for twenty million years, and the warm-blooded twist that made it unstoppable, then doomed it when the world went cold. Could one still be hiding in a trench? Ellie desperately wants yes. The three walls that say no are annoyingly solid.

The Giants Swimming Right Now

Then the giants that are swimming tonight, with names and trackers: Contender, the largest male great white ever tagged in the Atlantic, pinging north toward Cape Cod. Big Rose, twenty feet and three tons off Nova Scotia. Deep Blue, fifty years old and still out there. And Alpha — a nine-foot great white whose tag recorded the moment something bigger swallowed her whole, and dove nineteen hundred feet into the dark.

"The ocean is stranger, older, and kinder than the movies made us think. And the map still has edges."

Older Than Trees, and a Sound We Can't Explain

Plus a shark older than trees, a shark older than the telescope, and the gentle hundred-foot giant that quietly outlived them all. And in Rumors & Whispers: the deep-sea sounds we still can't fully explain — including a brand-new answer to a mystery that's haunted people for fifty years.
