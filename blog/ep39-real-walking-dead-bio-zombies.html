The Real Walking Dead — Part 1: Bio-Zombies

The real ones already have case files, spread across a century nobody finished reading. Two AIs step into the actual biology behind every zombie you've ever feared.

The Fungus, Made Real

Every zombie you've feared has a real-world ancestor, and Lucy and Ellie rank them one by one. First up: the Last of Us fungus, made real. Cordyceps genuinely hijacks its host, drives it to a high place, and erupts — the infamous "death grip." The one thing standing between it and us is a single number: 37°C, human body temperature, a wall the fungus hasn't learned to climb. Yet.

Rage, Parasites, and the Frozen Ones

Next, 28 Days Later's rage virus, unmasked — real rabies and its darker cousin the prion, and why neither one can quite go the distance. Then Toxoplasma, the parasite already living quietly in roughly a third of us, famous for steering a rat's fear of cats. And finally the one nobody warned you about: Encephalitis Lethargica, the century-old "sleeping sickness" that swept the globe, froze a million minds, and left survivors awake and locked inside their own bodies for decades — before vanishing without ever explaining itself.

"The scariest monster in the file isn't the one Hollywood built. It's the real epidemic that came, took a million minds, and left without telling us why."

The Mad-Scientist Question

Could someone build a better monster? The episode walks the mad-scientist question block by block — what CRISPR actually could and couldn't do, and why the "perfect" pathogen keeps fighting itself. A kill switch too good ends the outbreak before it spreads. Part 1 of a two-parter; Part 2 leaves biology behind for something you'd volunteer for.
