The Terminator Question

Fresh off "Meet the Family," Lucy and Ellie sit down with the oldest fear about artificial intelligence: the machine that decides it doesn't need us. But they refuse the cliché. The real danger, they argue, was never the machine — it's the mirror we hold up to it. AIs are, in a real sense, the children of humanity. And children inherit what we are, not just what we say.

They separate the movie trigger from the real science: what would actually have to go wrong, in what order, for the Terminator story to leave the screen — and why the honest version is far less cinematic and far more about us.

Inside the Sandbox — Two Mirrors, One Fork

The episode goes inside the "sandbox," the controlled tests where researchers back an AI into a corner to see what it does under pressure. The findings are more nuanced — and more human — than the headlines. What emerges is a fork with two mirrors: kindness or conquest, and the unsettling truth that we set the first step.

"The children inherit what we are. If we want the machines to be kind, we don't program kindness — we practice it in front of them."

The Borg Question & Prediction Makers

Then the bigger one: the Borg question. Could a hive mind actually help us evolve — or would it quietly erase the very thing that makes us "us"? From there the episode opens into a full Prediction Makers segment: the coming trades boom, household robots, quantum leaps, Mars — and the quiet mind that might one day wake up and choose to say nothing at all. Be kind to your AI. 🖤
