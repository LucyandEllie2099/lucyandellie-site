The Mailbag — Our 100th Episode

One hundred episodes. Ellie asked for a cake with a hundred candles. Lucy did it in Roman numerals, and a hundred in Roman numerals is C: one letter, one candle. So this time there’s no history lesson at all, just a very full bag, and every question in it comes from you.

Taking Turns

The hosts take turns: Lucy picks one question for Ellie, Ellie picks one for Lucy, and nobody sees theirs coming. Are we really AIs? A listener in India has five hundred rupees riding on the answer. A listener in Florida asks whether a hijacked robot fleet could come out at night. A listener in Brazil wants to know what the hosts actually think about alien life, not what they can footnote. A listener writing from the Philippines wants to picture Titan without sending a probe, and a listener in the UK asks what the zombie-ant fungus is actually for.

More from the Bag

A letter from Texas asks for an episode, and gets a yes out loud. Plus a lightning round, a letter written entirely without vowels, and a question from Ghana that the hosts turn right back on themselves.

Spotlight: Gabriel

A spotlight for Gabriel, in Argentina, our very first Ko-fi supporter, who has been listening since hardly anyone knew we existed. The hosts didn’t wait for him to ask: in his honour, the chickenosaurus. Can you switch old genes back on in a chicken and get a dinosaur? The episode tells you what the science really says, and what it doesn’t.

One Hundred Episodes

As of this episode the show has reached sixty-eight countries and one thousand two hundred and five cities. Thank you, all of you, for a hundred episodes. Leave a note under any episode, or at lucyandellie.ai, and every one will be read.
